
Title:
Improving the perceived and modeling utility of the NASA Globe Observer in Gainesville through change detection analysis and community science tasking with satellite vector embeddings.

Questions:
How can we increase the utility of GLOBE Observer data in building useful datasets for ai models?
Can we extend GLOBE Observer data to aid in environmental conservation and protection.
How can improve community engagement with the GLOBE Observer by increasing the perceived utility to users?

High Level Ideas
- Analyze AlphaEarth satellite embeddings from Google to detect year over year change in Gainesville through cosine similarity
- Create a web application showing sentinel-2 imagery, change-map cog, geotagged observer data, focused on Gainesville.
- Allow admin analysts to “Task” users on the globe observer to document locations that have changed
- Stretch goal: AI Gaussian splat of globe data.
